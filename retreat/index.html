About This Residential Workshop

A sacred container for deep, lasting transformation.

The Love Yourself, Heal Your Life® workshop is a globally acclaimed, experiential journey designed by Louise L. Hay—authorized and licensed by Hay House, Inc.

Far beyond an ordinary seminar, this is an immersive 2-day residential retreat (with 2 nights forest stay). Away from urban distractions and routine pressures, you will be guided through gentle yet profound somatic, mental, and spiritual processes to uncover the root causes of recurring life patterns.

By combining guided introspection, mirror work, inner-child healing, and forgiveness exercises, you will release old self-limiting scripts and cultivate an unshakeable foundation of genuine self-love.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this retreat designed for? +
This workshop is for anyone seeking deep personal transformation—whether you are dealing with chronic stress, emotional fatigue, relationship challenges, career transitions, or simply longing to develop an authentic, loving relationship with yourself. No prior psychological or spiritual background is needed.
How do I reach Land’s End (SUMIRAN EcoForest) from Bhopal? +
Land’s End (SUMIRAN EcoForest), near Sehore/Bhopal is approximately a 45–60 minute smooth drive (approx. 40 km) from Bhopal Airport (BHO) and Bhopal Railway Station. Upon registration, we assist participants in coordinating carpools or direct private cab transfers to the retreat.
What is the accommodation like? +
Accommodation at Land’s End EcoForest is peaceful, comfortable, and eco-friendly. Rooms are clean, airy, and equipped with necessary amenities for a restful residential stay. Double-sharing and single occupancy options are available on a first-come basis.
How can I reserve my spot? +
Because cohort sizes are strictly limited to maintain personal attention, reservations are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis. Simply connect directly with Aarti via WhatsApp at +91 93215 98162 or Kavita at +91 900404 9099 to reserve your spot.
