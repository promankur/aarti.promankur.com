Project Blossom (Formerly Project Survive)

From COVID-19 Survival Support to Nurturing Potential
Support via GPay 9321598162

The COVID-19 pandemic was a transformative experience for Healing Circle. To provide succour to the socio-economically challenged strata of society, Project Survive was initiated providing education, food, and essentials.

With your continuous contributions, our journey has transpired into Project Blossom. Over the past few years, we have focused on empowering street and underprivileged children by supporting their academic, nutritional, medical, and emotional well-being.
